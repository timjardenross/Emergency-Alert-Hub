Method

How the ratings are derived and how to use the site list.

Rating logic

  • High: direct overlap with elevated fire potential or likely access disruption.
  • Moderate: nearby exposure or plausible spillover impact.
  • Watch: monitor because conditions could deteriorate quickly.
  • Low: limited expected impact, retained for footprint completeness.

Impact categories

  • Customer access
  • Staff mobility
  • Branch trading
  • Digital and call-centre demand
  • Power, comms, smoke and road disruption

Public-location caveat

  • Locations are indicative public-facing sites only.
  • They are not an internal asset or property register.
  • Use live locators before external distribution or operational action.
